📚 About StudyBuddy

Connecting learners worldwide, one study session at a time

🎯 Our Mission

StudyBuddy was born from a simple idea: learning is better together. We believe that everyone deserves access to collaborative learning environments where they can connect with like-minded students, share knowledge, and achieve their academic goals.

🌟 Our Story

Founded in 2024 by a group of passionate developers and educators, StudyBuddy started as a small project to help university students find study partners. Today, we've grown into a global community of over 10,000 active learners across 50+ countries.
