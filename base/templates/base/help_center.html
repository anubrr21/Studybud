Frequently Asked Questions

📝 Account & Profile

How do I create an account? ▼
Click "Sign Up" in the top right, enter your email and username, and choose a password. You'll receive a verification code to confirm your email.
How do I reset my password? ▼
Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your email, and we'll send you a reset link. Click it to create a new password.
How do I change my profile picture? ▼
Go to your profile, click "Edit Profile," then upload a new image. We recommend square images for best results.

💬 Rooms & Messaging

How do I create a study room? ▼
Click the "+ Create Room" button on the homepage. Choose a topic, name your room, and add a description. You're ready to go!
How do I send a private message? ▼
Visit any user's profile and click the "Message" button. This starts a private chat between you two.
Can I delete messages? ▼
Yes! Hover over any message you sent to see delete options. You can delete for yourself or for everyone.

🔔 Notifications

What notifications will I receive? ▼
You'll be notified when someone follows you, likes your room, comments in your room, or sends you a message.
How do I mark notifications as read? ▼
Click the bell icon to see notifications. Clicking any notification marks it as read. Use "Mark all as read" button.

🎨 Chat Themes

How do I change chat theme? ▼
In any private chat, click the theme button in the top right. Choose from 30+ beautiful themes!
